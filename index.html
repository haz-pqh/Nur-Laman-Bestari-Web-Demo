About Nur Eco Resort Group

Second-to-Home Eco Sanctuaries in Peninsular Malaysia

NUR ECO RESORT GROUP brings together scenic eco-resorts across Peninsular Malaysia surrounded by lush tropical rainforests, crystal-clear stony rivers, waterfalls, ponds, lakes, hills, and valleys — offering a true back-to-nature escape right near the city.

With comfortable accommodations, well-equipped seminar halls and training rooms, and land, air, and water adventures, we host individuals, families, students, government agencies, and corporate groups for teambuilding courses, seminars, retreats, and family days.
